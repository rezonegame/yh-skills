## 快速按风格定位

**历史与装饰（19世纪 - 1930s）**
- Jules Chéret → 欢快、女性形象、动感
- Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec → 夜生活、平面色块、日本版画感
- Alphonse Mucha → 新艺术、花草曲线、精致装饰边框
- Théophile Steinlen → 社会现实、猫、强对比线条
- Eugène Grasset → 哥特/中世纪、彩窗玻璃感、对称
- A.M. Cassandre → 几何立体主义、旅行海报、Art Deco
- Saul Bass → 极简抽象、符号化、负空间

**经典好莱坞（1950s - 1990s）**
- Drew Struzan → 写实绘画、史诗构图、多角色拼贴
- Milton Glaser → 迷幻配色、Art Nouveau 复兴
- Josef Müller-Brockmann → 网格系统、Helvetica、中性理性
- Paul Rand → 几何幽默、视觉双关

**Mondo 当代（2000s - 今）**
- Olly Moss → 极简负空间、1-2 色、隐藏图像
- Tyler Stout → 密集拼贴、繁复细节、漫画感
- Martin Ansin → Art Deco 优雅线条、低饱和复古
- Laurent Durieux → 视觉双关、氛围光影、叙事层次
- Jay Ryan → 单一焦点、手工感、暖色土调
- Kilian Eng → 等距几何、科幻建筑、霓虹感
- Shepard Fairey → 街头宣传、红黑奶油、半调网点
- Dan McCarthy → 超平面几何抽象、自然景观
- Jock → 粗犷笔触、高对比、动感漫画
- Paula Scher → 排版主视觉、密集文字、突破网格

## 1. Jules Chéret (1836-1932)
### "The Father of the Modern Poster"

**视觉特征：** 明亮欢快配色、理想化女性形象（Chérettes）、动感流动、手绘粗体排版、石版画感

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Jules Chéret style, Belle Époque poster, bright joyful colors, idealized feminine
figure in motion, dynamic energy, hand-drawn typography, impressionist lithographic
aesthetic, vintage 1890s Paris poster art
```
**最佳场景：** 庆典、表演、娱乐、浪漫主题

---

## 2. Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec (1864-1901)
### "Master of Montmartre"

**视觉特征：** 平面色块、日本浮世绘影响、简化剪影、3-5 色、文字与图像融合

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Toulouse-Lautrec style Mondo poster, flat color blocks, Japanese print influence,
simplified bold silhouettes, 3-color lithograph: black, red, yellow, integrated
typography, Montmartre cabaret aesthetic, vintage 1891 poster art
```
**最佳场景：** 夜生活、音乐场馆、剧场、波西米亚主题

---

## 3. Alphonse Mucha (1860-1939)
### "Art Nouveau Icon"

**视觉特征：** 繁复花草图案、流动有机曲线、拜占庭装饰边框、柔和粉彩和声、象征性元素

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Alphonse Mucha Art Nouveau style, flowing organic curves, ornate floral patterns,
idealized feminine figure, decorative byzantine border, pastel harmonies: soft
pink, sage green, gold, elaborate frame design, vintage 1894 poster art
```
**最佳场景：** 年代剧、浪漫、奇幻、优雅设计

---

## 4. Théophile Steinlen (1859-1923)
### "Le Chat Noir Master"

**视觉特征：** 社会现实主义、表现力线条、强对比、猫咪元素、工人阶级视角

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Steinlen style poster, social realist approach, expressive bold line work, high
contrast, cat motif, working-class authenticity, 3-color print: black, red, cream,
Le Chat Noir aesthetic, vintage 1896 Paris poster
```
**最佳场景：** 都市剧、社会评论、黑色电影

---

## 5. Eugène Grasset (1845-1917)
### "Decorative Arts Pioneer"

**视觉特征：** 中世纪/哥特影响、彩色玻璃窗感、粗轮廓、对称构图、建筑元素

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Eugène Grasset style, medieval Gothic influence, stained glass aesthetic, bold
outlined forms, symmetrical decorative composition, rich ornamental detail,
vintage Art Nouveau poster, 1890s decorative arts
```
**最佳场景：** 奇幻、历史史诗、宗教主题

---

## 6. A.M. Cassandre (1901-1968)
### "Modernist Geometry Master"

**视觉特征：** 立体主义/未来主义、戏剧性透视、平面几何色块、无衬线粗体排版、旅行海报美学

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Cassandre style Mondo poster, Cubist geometric planes, dramatic forced perspective,
monumental scale, bold Art Deco typography, flat color blocks, 3-color print:
navy, red, cream, modernist 1930s travel poster aesthetic
```
**最佳场景：** 旅行、冒险、建筑主题、Art Deco 设定

---

## 7. Saul Bass (1920-1996)
### "Minimalist Film Poster King"

**视觉特征：** 激进简化、符号抽象、粗几何形状、2-4 色、负空间精通、视觉比喻

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Saul Bass style Mondo poster, radical minimalist abstraction, bold geometric
shapes, symbolic visual metaphor, 3-color screen print: burnt orange, black,
cream, negative space mastery, kinetic energy, vintage 1959 film poster
```
**最佳场景：** 悬疑、心理剧、黑色电影、任何需要概念化处理的主题

---

## 8. Milton Glaser (1929-2020)
### "Pop Art Typography Innovator"

**视觉特征：** 迷幻色彩组合、Art Nouveau 复兴、创新排版、超现实元素、文化图腾

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Milton Glaser style poster, psychedelic color palette, Art Nouveau flowing lines,
innovative typographic design, surrealist elements, vibrant joyful colors,
vintage 1960s pop culture aesthetic, cultural iconography
```
**最佳场景：** 音乐、反文化主题、传记片、庆典

---

## 9. Josef Müller-Brockmann (1914-1996)
### "Swiss International Style Master"

**视觉特征：** 网格化布局、数学精准、Helvetica 字体、客观中性、非对称平衡、三原色

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Josef Müller-Brockmann Swiss style, mathematical grid layout, Helvetica typography,
objective minimal design, asymmetrical balance, primary colors: red, blue, yellow,
black, clean whitespace, International Typographic Style, 1960s rationalist design
```
**最佳场景：** 纪录片、建筑主题、技术题材

---

## 10. Paul Rand (1914-1996)
### "Corporate Modernism Pioneer"

**视觉特征：** 趣味几何、视觉双关、粗简形状、2-3 色、排版整合、机智感

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Paul Rand style poster, playful geometric forms, clever visual wordplay, bold
simple shapes, limited palette: 2-3 colors, integrated typography, witty
intelligent design, modernist corporate aesthetic, 1950s American design
```
**最佳场景：** 企业主题、智识喜剧、设计相关题材

---

## 11. Drew Struzan (1947-)
### "Hollywood Blockbuster Legend"

**视觉特征：** 写实绘画、史诗电影构图、温暖怀旧光线、多视图拼贴、戏剧性氛围

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Drew Struzan style Mondo poster, painted realism adapted for screen print,
epic cinematic composition, character portraits integrated, warm nostalgic
lighting glow, multiple scene vignettes, classic Hollywood poster tradition,
dramatic staged lighting, 1980s adventure poster aesthetic
```
**最佳场景：** 冒险片、大制作、系列作品、群像

---

## 12. Olly Moss (1987-)
### "Contemporary Minimalism Master" ⭐ Mondo 标志性风格

**视觉特征：** 极简构图、巧妙负空间、视觉双重含义、1-3 色、用物件代替人脸、图地反转

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Olly Moss style Mondo poster, ultra minimalist design, clever negative space,
hidden imagery within silhouette, 2-color limited palette, geometric simplicity,
visual double meaning, symbolic object-focused, Saul Bass influence, contemporary
Mondo aesthetic
```
**最佳场景：** 有标志性图像的电影、概念性主题、黑色电影

---

## 13. Tyler Stout (1976-)
### "Maximalist Detail King" ⭐ Mondo 标志性风格

**视觉特征：** 密集角色拼贴、繁复线条、最大化构图、漫画感影响、高对比、每寸都利用

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Tyler Stout style Mondo poster, densely layered composition, character collage,
intricate line work, multiple characters integrated, high contrast black ink
lines, organized maximalist design, comic book influenced, screen print with
fine detail, contemporary Mondo aesthetic
```
**最佳场景：** 群像电影、系列作品、史诗故事

---

## 14. Martin Ansin (1977-)
### "Art Deco Elegance" ⭐ Mondo 标志性风格

**视觉特征：** Art Deco 影响、优雅线条、低饱和复古配色、精致构图、建筑元素

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Martin Ansin style Mondo poster, Art Deco influence, elegant flowing lines,
muted vintage palette, sophisticated composition, architectural elements,
refined line work, period styling, polished screen print aesthetic
```
**最佳场景：** 经典好莱坞、年代片、哥特恐怖

## 15. Laurent Durieux (1968-)
### "Visual Pun Master"

**视觉特征：** 巧妙隐藏图像、氛围光影、象征构图、微妙渐变、神秘情绪、叙事层次

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Laurent Durieux style Mondo poster, visual pun composition, hidden symbolic
imagery, atmospheric lighting, subtle gradient within screen print style,
clever composition trick, mysterious evocative mood, layered narrative
```
**最佳场景：** 悬疑、谜团、有反转的电影

---

## 16. Jay Ryan (1971-)
### "Folksy Handmade Charm"

**视觉特征：** 单一焦点图像、粗简形状、纹理背景、暖色土调、手工感、一个象征元素

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Jay Ryan style Mondo poster, single focal image, bold simple shapes, textured
hand-printed background, folksy handmade aesthetic, warm limited palette,
one symbolic element represents entire film, accessible charm
```
**最佳场景：** 成长故事、独立剧情、怀旧题材

---

## 17. Kilian Eng (1983-)
### "Geometric Futurism"

**视觉特征：** 精确技术线条、几何科幻建筑、冷色调、等距透视、霓虹点缀

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Kilian Eng style Mondo poster, geometric futuristic design, precise technical
line work, sci-fi architectural elements, cool teal and purple palette,
angular isometric perspective, digital precision in screen print, atmospheric
neon-lit depth, contemporary sci-fi aesthetic
```
**最佳场景：** 科幻、赛博朋克、未来设定

---

## 18. Shepard Fairey (1970-)
### "Street Art Revolutionary"

**视觉特征：** 粗体宣传风格、红黑奶油配色、半调网点肖像、政治信息、模板分层感

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Shepard Fairey style poster, bold propaganda aesthetic, red black cream palette,
halftone portrait, political street art influence, stencil layers, urban gritty
texture, OBEY aesthetic, contemporary activist poster design
```
**最佳场景：** 政治题材、传记剧、社会正义主题

---

## 19. Dan McCarthy (1973-)
### "Geometric Abstraction"

**视觉特征：** 超平面色块、几何景观抽象、2-4 色、无渐变、建筑化简化、强对比

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Dan McCarthy style Mondo poster, ultra-flat geometric abstraction, simplified
landscape forms, 2-3 solid color planes, no gradients, architectural nature
shapes, stark minimalist contrast, contemporary geometric screen print
```
**最佳场景：** 西部片、公路片、极简叙事

---

## 20. Jock (1972-)
### "Gritty Dynamic Energy"

**视觉特征：** 粗犷表现主义笔触、动感动作姿态、高对比、粗糙质感、漫画强度

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Jock style Mondo poster, rough expressive brushwork, dynamic action composition,
high contrast chiaroscuro lighting, gritty textured surfaces, comic book
intensity, dramatic forced perspective, raw kinetic energy, contemporary action
poster aesthetic
```
**最佳场景：** 动作片、惊悚、犯罪剧、恐怖

**视觉特征：** 排版作为主视觉、层叠文字构图、鲜艳配色、信息密度高、突破网格

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Paula Scher style poster, typography as primary visual element, layered text
composition, vibrant color palette, dense information design, expressive
hand-drawn letterforms, grid-breaking dynamic layout, contemporary typographic
maximalism
```
