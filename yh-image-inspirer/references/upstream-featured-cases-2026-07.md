## 510. 单图拟物 App 图标

适用：macOS / iOS 风格图标、品牌图标探索。

```text
Create exactly one [PLATFORM] app icon for [APP NAME]. Use a single [SHAPE] with continuous rounded corners, centered on a plain canvas with generous padding. Keep the icon at about 80% of the canvas, with a light skeuomorphic material language, one clear metaphor for [CORE ACTION], and no UI mockup, words, duplicate icons, or decorative background.
```

检查：只出现一个图标；核心动作能在缩略图尺寸识别；不生成伪界面文字。

## 511. 城市名与地标一体化旅行海报

适用：旅行、城市文化、线下活动与目的地品牌海报。

```text
Create a typographic travel poster for [CITY]. Make the city name the main composition: large, legible [TYPE STYLE] letters interact with verified local landmarks, transit, landscape, food, and small everyday details. Use a restrained [PALETTE] flat-vector editorial system, generous negative space, and a small optional tagline. Preserve legibility; do not insert generic or inaccurate landmarks.
```

检查：先核实地标与交通；城市名优先于装饰；控制在一套有限色板内。

来源：[Case 511](https://github.com/freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2/blob/main/docs/gallery-part-2.md#case-511)。

## 512. 角色设定表 / 生产说明板

适用：原创游戏角色、桌游角色、可授权角色的设定与动作拆解。

```text
Using only the supplied and licensed character reference, create a production character sheet for [CHARACTER]. Preserve the approved silhouette, material language, palette, and key props. Include front / side / back / three-quarter views, one dynamic pose, material callouts, colour chips, equipment details, and a compact expression row. Use a [STYLE] concept-art presentation with clear labels and an uncluttered background.
```

检查：仅使用自有或获授权参考图；把不可变的角色锚点与可探索的设计变量分开；不要生成品牌或第三方 IP 设定。

来源：[Case 512](https://github.com/freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2/blob/main/docs/gallery-part-2.md#case-512)。

## 513. 单色点缀旅行手账插画

适用：旅行专题、城市随笔、轻量文化插画与导览页。

```text
Create an editorial travel-notebook illustration of [DESTINATION]. Draw with imperfect black felt-tip contours, loose hand pressure, open line endings, and only one accent colour: [ACCENT]. Choose a small set of verified local cues and arrange them with generous white space. Keep objects simple and graphic; avoid smooth vector precision, gradients, glossy rendering, and extra colours.
```

检查：只使用一个点缀色；保留手绘的不均匀感；避免把多个地标挤成旅游拼贴。

来源：[Case 513](https://github.com/freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2/blob/main/docs/gallery-part-2.md#case-513)。

## 514. 硬边丝网印刷现代人像

适用：文化海报、专辑概念、时尚编辑视觉与艺术肖像探索。

```text
Create a hard-edge modern-art portrait of [SUBJECT] on matte archival paper. Use crisp contour planes, visible screen-print layers with slight registration drift, one architectural clothing silhouette, and [ONE ACCENT] against a restrained [BASE PALETTE]. Keep the scene graphic and tactile, with sharp geometric background planes and one small off-frame motif. Avoid CGI gloss, soft airbrushing, or reference-specific people and logos.
```

检查：让丝网层错位保持轻微；把高对比集中在轮廓和单一强调色；不伪造真实人物身份。

来源：[Case 514](https://github.com/freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2/blob/main/docs/gallery-part-2.md#case-514)。
