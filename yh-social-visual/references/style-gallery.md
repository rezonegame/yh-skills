# Content Visual Style Gallery

Offer a compact style choice only when the user has not supplied a visual direction and the choice materially changes the package. Recommend at most three directions derived from content tone and platform context.

| Direction | Character | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean editorial | Structured grid, restrained color, precise hierarchy | Analysis, business, technology, explainers |
| Warm narrative | Human scale, tactile texture, softer palette | Personal stories, lifestyle, education |
| Bold typographic | Large type, high contrast, graphic shapes | Opinion, launch, social hooks, cover-led packages |
| Diagrammatic | Modular components, labels, arrows, visual evidence | Tutorials, systems, comparisons, frameworks |
| Cinematic image-led | Strong scene, atmosphere, negative space | Culture, travel, interviews, feature stories |

Lock one primary direction for the package. Introduce a second related family only when cover and inline-image roles require different treatments.
